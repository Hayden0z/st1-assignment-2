1. Problem summary
Write approximately 100 words.
SmartCare Community Clinic currently relies on spreadsheets and paper records to manage patients, practitioners, and appointments. This manual approach is slow, has possible errors and makes it difficult to maintain accurate information. Staff struggle with locating records, preventing double‑bookings, and ensuring data consistency across multiple files. As the clinic grows, these limitations create operational inefficiencies and increase the risk of lost or incomplete patient information. The client needs a software system that centralises patient and practitioner data, streamlines appointment scheduling, reduces administrative workload, and improves reliability. A digital solution will support better record‑keeping, faster access to information, and overall clinic efficiency.
